

Here are commands to test bots against eachother with fastchess:

fastchess \
  -engine cmd=./target/release/ui name=A \
  -engine cmd=./target/release/ui name=B \
  -each nodes=100000 \
  -openings file=/home/thomas-paulen/stockfish-books/UHO_Lichess_4852_v1.epd format=epd order=random \
  -srand 12345 \
  -rounds 20 \
  -repeat \
  -concurrency 1 \
  -pgnout file=selftest.pgn
